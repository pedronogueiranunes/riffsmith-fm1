RIFFSMITH

Guitar firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Turn the FM-1 into a guitar for indie, metal and reggae. Six synthesised strings, strummed, palm-muted, bent and choked live, through amps and a pedalboard, on a four-track riffbox with drums and bass. No samples needed.
